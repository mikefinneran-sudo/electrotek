Areas of Expertise

Forensic analysis and expert opinion for matters involving electrical systems, product failures, and fire causation.

Origin & Cause Support

Detailed analysis to determine whether an electrical device was the cause of ignition or a victim of the fire. Arc mapping, conductor examination, and failure mode identification following NFPA 921 methodology.

Product Failure Analysis

Examination of appliances and electrical components to identify manufacturing defects, design flaws, or installation errors. From small appliances to distribution panels.

Expert Witness Testimony

Deposition and trial testimony for both plaintiff and defense. Consistently admitted in federal and state courts across the country.

Vehicle Fire Investigation

CVFI-certified analysis of passenger vehicles, commercial trucks, and agricultural equipment. 12V systems, alternator failures, battery incidents, and after-market wiring.
