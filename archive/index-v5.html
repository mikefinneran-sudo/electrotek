Areas of Expertise

Forensic analysis and expert opinions involving fire origin and cause, product failures, vehicle fires, and electrical systems.

Origin & Cause Investigations

Systematic examination of fire scenes to determine the point of origin and cause of ignition. Our analysis includes arc mapping, conductor examination, electrical component evaluation, and failure mode identification — conducted in accordance with NFPA 921 methodology.

Product Failure Analysis

In-depth examination and testing of electrical products, appliances, and components involved in fire or failure incidents. We determine whether manufacturing defects, design flaws, or installation errors contributed to the event — from residential appliances to commercial distribution systems.

Vehicle Fire Investigation

CVFI-certified analysis of passenger vehicles, commercial trucks, and agricultural equipment. 12V systems, alternator failures, battery incidents, and after-market wiring.

Expert Testimony

Deposition and trial testimony as expert witnesses in matters involving electrical fires, product failures, and vehicle fire origins. We accept assignments for both defense and plaintiff.
