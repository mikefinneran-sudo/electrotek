Areas of Expertise

What We Investigate

01

Origin & Cause Investigations

Systematic examination of fire scenes to determine the area of origin and identify potential ignition sources. We collect and survey physical evidence, conduct control testing, and perform electrical component examination — all in accordance with NFPA 921 methodology.
02

Product Failure Analysis

In-depth evaluation and testing of electrical products, components, and appliances. We determine potential failure modes such as defects, flaws, and construction errors — from residential appliances to commercial distribution systems.
03

Vehicle Fire Investigation

CVFI-certified analysis of passenger vehicles, commercial trucks, and agricultural equipment. Investigations include examinations of 12V systems, alternators, batteries, battery conduits, and aftermarket wiring.
04

Expert Witness Testimony

Deposition and trial testimony in matters involving electrical fires, product failures, and vehicle fire origins. We accept expert witness assignments for both plaintiff and defense counsel.

“Independent, unbiased forensic analysis for insurance carriers, attorneys, and manufacturers — serving the industry for over thirty years.”
