As a Purdue University graduate with a Bachelor of Science Degree in Electrical Engineering Technology and numerous professional affiliations, Jim offers clients a wide range of technical and professional knowledge.

Professional Certifications

CFEI – Certified Fire and Explosion Investigator

Issued by the National Association of Fire Investigators (NAFI). This credential requires passing a rigorous examination based on NFPA 921 and asserts competency in the scientific method as applied to fire analysis.

IAAI-CFI – Certified Fire Investigator

Issued by the International Association of Arson Investigators (IAAI). This credential certifies competency in fire investigation methodology and scene analysis, requiring demonstrated experience and examination.

CVFI – Certified Vehicle Fire Investigator

Specialized endorsement indicating advanced competency in the unique electrical and mechanical systems of automobiles, including 12V/high-voltage DC electrical systems, fuel loads, and lithium-ion battery hazards.

NFPA Technical Committee

NFPA 921: Guide for Fire and Explosion Investigations

Mr. Finneran served as a Committee Member on the NFPA 921 Technical Committee from 2004 through 2018—the standard-setting body for fire and explosion investigation methodology. NFPA 921 is widely regarded as the definitive standard for the fire investigation profession and is frequently cited in legal proceedings to qualify expert testimony.

His role involved developing and refining scientific protocols for fire scene documentation, evidence analysis, and standardizing the criteria for identifying electrical ignition sources.

Instruction & Curriculum Development

Fire Findings Laboratory

Mr. Finneran served as the Lead Electrical Instructor at Fire Findings, a highly respected laboratory and training center in the forensic investigation industry, for approximately 28 years. Fire Findings discontinued its seminar program during COVID.

“Residential Electricity for Fire Investigators”

He authored and co-presented this seminar alongside Jack L. Sanderson (a renowned author on fire causation) and Nathan P. Dwyer, PE. The curriculum bridged the gap between general fire investigation and specialized electrical engineering.
