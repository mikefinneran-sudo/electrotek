James V. Miller is a forensic fire investigator specializing in origin and cause determination and product failure analysis of components involved in fires. He joined ElectroTek Consultants in 2000 and holds degrees in Industrial Engineering Technology, Mechanical Engineering Technology, and an Automotive Technician diploma.

James also served as a volunteer firefighter and Captain with Northeast Fire and EMS (2000–2018), giving him firsthand fire suppression experience that enhances his forensic analysis.

Issued by the National Association of Fire Investigators (NAFI). This credential requires passing a rigorous examination based on NFPA 921 and asserts competency in the scientific method as applied to fire analysis.
